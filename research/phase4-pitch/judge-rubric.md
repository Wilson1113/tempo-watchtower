# Judge rubric — tempo-watchtower pitch deck (score out of 100, pass = 90)

The judge plays a Colosseum judging-panel member (Colosseum staff + a Tempo-track ecosystem judge)
reviewing a hackathon pitch deck. Score each category, justify each score in 1-3 sentences, and
list concrete fixes. Be harsh: a 90+ deck should be one you'd shortlist for a 15-minute interview.

## A. Official contest criteria (rules §8) — 48 pts (8 each)
1. Functionality — does the deck make a credible, specific case the product works (demo proof, code
   quality signals, real testnet transactions), not just a concept?
2. Potential Impact — TAM + impact on the broader crypto ecosystem, argued honestly (no inflated
   numbers) but convincingly (growth mechanism, why it matters beyond today's size).
3. Novelty — is the uniqueness clear and defensible (competitive gap stated precisely)?
4. UX — how blockchain creates great UX for downstream users (here: payee operators).
5. Open-source / composability — how it composes with Tempo/MPP primitives and the Rust ecosystem.
6. Business Plan — viable business + team's ability to execute (solo builder: how is this credible?).

## B. Colosseum internal dimensions — 28 pts (4 each)
Founder-market fit · Insight (non-obvious truth) · Product + execution · Market size ·
Founder communication (clarity, concision) · Viability · Traction (what hackathon-stage evidence
of demand exists or is planned).

## C. Pitch craft — 16 pts
- (4) Follows patterns of past Colosseum winners and official pitch guidance
  (research/phase4-pitch/winner-patterns.md).
- (4) Hook: the first slide/15 seconds makes a judge care (named user + concrete loss).
- (4) Narrative flow: each slide earns the next; fits a 2-3 minute pitch video.
- (4) Honesty: every factual claim traceable to the evidence files; no fabricated traction,
  customers, or numbers; placeholders clearly marked where the founder must fill in.

## D. Build-readiness for an AI deck generator — 8 pts
- (4) Each slide has: title, on-slide text (short), speaker notes, and a visual suggestion.
- (4) Self-contained: an AI slide tool could build the deck from this file alone.

## Output format
Total /100, per-item scores, top 5 highest-leverage fixes (ordered), and any factual claims you
could not trace to the source files (list them).

Source files the judge must check claims against:
- research/phase2-decks/tempo-watchtower.md (verified deck content + evidence appendix)
- research/phase3-verification/tempo-watchtower-verdict.md
- research/phase4-pitch/winner-patterns.md
- research/00-brief.md (rules, prizes, judging criteria)
